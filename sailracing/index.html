SR 100
PROTECTION VEST

Developed together with Alinghi Red Bull Racing, challenger of the 37th America's Cup. The SR100 Protection Vest is carefully developed for high-speed sailing and engineered to offer excellent collision protection and freedom of movement.

Constructed in line with the 37th America's Cup protocol regulations, it provides 50N buoyancy. The protection vest is made from 23% elastane and 49% nylon. Features an integrated 4 mm XT D3O Impact Protection in the front and over the collarbone, as well as covering the back, safeguarding you during high-impact maneuvers. The back protection is removable and the side panels have no impact material, for flexibility.
